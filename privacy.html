Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Michael Hyun, a licensed California REALTOR® (DRE# 02442479) operating as BlueDoor Realty LLC and brokered by Compass (“we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, and protects information when you visit michaelhyunn.com (the “Site”) or contact us. By using the Site or submitting a form, you agree to this Policy.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, phone number, property or area of interest, and any message you send through our contact, home-valuation, or newsletter forms. We also automatically collect limited technical and usage data — such as your IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and referring source — through cookies and similar technologies and our analytics tools.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide real estate services.
  • To contact you by email, phone, and — if you provide a mobile number — text message about your request, listings, valuations, appointments, and related updates.
  • To send our newsletter and market updates, if you opt in (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Site.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

SMS terms & conditions

By providing your mobile phone number on one of our forms, you consent to receive calls and text messages (including via automated technology) from Michael Hyun · BlueDoor Realty LLC regarding your inquiry and our real estate services. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. The following terms apply to our SMS program:

  1. We use SMS to confirm appointments, send reminders, and notify clients of schedule updates or important changes.
  2. You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Just text “STOP”. After you send the SMS message “STOP” to us, we will send you an SMS message to confirm that you have been unsubscribed. After this, you will no longer receive SMS messages from us. If you want to join again, just sign up as you did the first time, and we will start sending SMS messages to you again.
  3. If you are experiencing issues with the messaging program, you can reply with the keyword “HELP” for more assistance, or you can get help directly at michael.hyun@compass.com.
  4. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  5. As always, message and data rates may apply for any messages sent to you from us and to us from you. Message frequency may vary. If you have any questions about your text plan or data plan, it is best to contact your wireless provider.
  6. If you have any questions regarding privacy, please read our privacy policy: https://www.michaelhyunn.com/privacy

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate our business — such as our customer relationship management system (GoHighLevel), our form-delivery provider, our website host (Vercel), and our brokerage (Compass) — and only as needed to provide our services or as required by law.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the categories of information described above exclude text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Cookies & analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies (including a website visitor-tracking pixel) to understand how visitors use the Site and to improve your experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some Site features.

Your choices

  • Email: unsubscribe using the link in any newsletter, or contact us.
  • Text messages: reply STOP to opt out at any time.
  • Access & deletion: you may request that we access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing us.

California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), including the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion, and to opt out of any “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Policy
