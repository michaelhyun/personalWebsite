The Buyer Experience

Seven steps from “just looking” to keys in hand.

01

Discovery & Strategy

We start with your goals, timeline, and finances — including RSUs and equity comp. You leave with a clear plan and a realistic budget you’re confident in.

02

Financing & Pre-Approval

I connect you with trusted lenders and help you secure a strong pre-approval, so your offers carry real weight when it counts.

03

Curated Home Search

Real-time listings tailored to your criteria — schools, commute, value — plus off-market opportunities through the Compass network.

04

Tours & Evaluation

We tour with intention. I read disclosures, flag red flags, and pull comps so you understand exactly what each home is — and isn’t — worth.

05

Offer & Negotiation

A data-backed offer strategy designed to win without overpaying. I negotiate hard on price, terms, and contingencies to protect your equity.

06

Escrow & Inspections

I quarterback inspections, appraisal, and timelines so nothing slips. You always know the next step and what it means.

07

Close & Beyond

Keys in hand — and a partner for life. I stay in your corner for vendors, equity reviews, and your next move whenever it comes.
